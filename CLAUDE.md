VINEYARD & PRODUCTION DETAILS

Climate Advantage

- Mediterranean climate: mild, warm, protected from cold winds  
- \- High luminosity ensures optimal grape ripening  
- \- Beneficial autumn/spring rainfall  
- \- Temperature range: January minimum to July maximum  
- \- Ideal conditions for both white and red varieties

Production Process

Harvest (Vendimia)

- Begins mid-to-late August  
- \- Weekly grape sampling (every 3-4 days) measuring acidity and sugar levels  
- \- Peak maturation point determination through data analysis

Elaboration (Bodega Facilities)

- Location: On-property at Masía Casa Gran  
- \- Modern equipment: Pneumatic press, peristaltic pumps, stainless steel tanks, heat exchangers  
- \- Tank volumes match parcel dimensions for terroir integrity  
- \- Temperature control: Critical throughout production  
- \- Process directed by enologist with focus on asepsis and quality

Aging & Crianza

- Location: Historic barrel room (underground) \+ 18th-century stone cellars  
- \- Temperature: Maintained 16-21°C year-round  
- \- Humidity: Constant levels optimal for maturation  
- \- White wines: Short barrel periods (350L oak)  
- \- Red wines: Extended traditional barrel aging in French oak (Nevers)  
- \- Final aging assessment: Regular tasting determines completion point

Environmental Responsibility

- Proper subproduct processing  
- \- Specialized treatment of vinazas (production wastewater)  
- \- Ecological certification standards
